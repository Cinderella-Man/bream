The twelve passed milestones are narrower than completing G1, G2, G3, G4 or G5. They use real native generated code and experimental public boundaries; they are not a published, general-purpose library. Frame layout, consuming ownership transfer and avoiding unnecessary queue-node allocation remain useful structural observations. All earlier speed measurements and derived claims were removed; they must be redone. Correctness progress does not select a production allowance or establish a latency guarantee.

## Next bounded milestone

Add checked application self-exit and targeted local exit for a restricted reason set. Derive normal, abnormal and explicit-kill behavior from pinned BEAM source, including trap handling, self-targets and dead or reused identities. Distinguish an explicit untrappable kill request from an ordinary propagated killed reason. Require exact Down/Exit behavior, no further application execution after committed self-exit, and settled held/queued shared ownership.

Keep the public reason vocabulary bounded; do not introduce arbitrary Rust exceptions, unrestricted Erlang terms or distribution. This advances remaining G4 obligations alongside partial G5 integration. General membership churn, real-time timers and full admission/resource qualification remain open. Responsiveness remains the user's chosen priority; use logical ordering and correctness assertions only, never elapsed peer/stop latency measurements here.

Source work remains driven by an unresolved obligation in the milestone, not by a goal of reading every BEAM function. Keep the broader source frontier available without expanding it as a substitute for executable progress.

Each future update should say what milestone passed, what failed or changed, which evidence supports it and what remains. The [full build gates](build-plan.md#build-order-and-exit-gates) remain authoritative for broader qualification.
